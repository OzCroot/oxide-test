01: The lighthouse keeper woke before dawn.
02: He lit the lamp and checked the glass.
03: Fog lay thick across the harbour.
04: A bell rang somewhere far off.
05: The tide was already turning.
06: He poured tea and watched the water.
07: Gulls argued on the rocks below.
08: A boat appeared at the edge of the fog.
09: It carried no lantern and no flag.
10: He raised the lamp and signalled twice.
11: The boat did not answer.
12: It drifted closer to the rocks.
13: He ran down the stairs, two at a time.
14: The rope was wet and heavy in his hands.
15: He threw it as far as he could.
16: The line fell short and sank.
17: He hauled it back and tried again.
18: This time a figure caught the rope.
19: Together they dragged the boat clear.
20: The stranger said nothing for a long while.
21: Then he asked for a name and a map.
22: The keeper gave him both.
23: By noon the fog had lifted.
24: The boat was gone by evening.
25: He lit the lamp again that night.
26: Nobody ever asked about it.
27: He never told a soul.
28: The sea kept its own counsel.
29: Years later he found a coin on the sill.
30: It was the last thing he understood.
